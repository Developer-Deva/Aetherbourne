# Aetherbourne — Systemic Emergent Narrative Overview

## Core Vision
Aetherbourne is a **2D top-down pixel-art life simulation** built around:

- **Systemic design**
- **Procedural generation**
- **Emergent storytelling**

Instead of scripted narratives, the world behaves like an interconnected machine: **unique stories emerge from actions, lived experiences, relationships, and internal needs**.

## Identity & Individuals
Every creature is an individual:

- Born with inherited **genetic traits** (physical characteristics + strengths/weaknesses)
- Maintains **needs, emotions, memories, and evolving personality**
- Develops over time based on:
  - genetics
  - experiences
  - relationships

## World Generation & Environmental Logic
The planet is procedurally generated from environmental factors such as:

- climate
- temperature
- humidity
- fertility
- drainage
- water access
- geological conditions

These inputs define:

- biomes
- resource availability
- survivability and challenges

Crucially, landscapes, ecosystems, hazards, and resources arise from a shared underlying environmental model (not isolated features).

## Resources & Ecosystems as Functional Systems
Flora and minerals are not decorative:

- generated as part of ecosystems
- vary in rarity, growth patterns, toxicity, medicinal properties
- influence survival, exploration, crafting, trade, and downstream future systems

## Motivation → Action → Emotion → Memory → Personality
A consistent loop drives the simulation’s “liveness”:

1. **Creatures are driven by needs** (biological + psychological)
2. Needs shape **goals**
3. Goals shape **actions**
4. Actions produce **events**
5. Events create **emotional responses**
6. Emotions become **memories** (especially repeated/salient experiences)
7. Memory repetition slowly drifts **personality**

## Relationships & Population-Level Evolution
Relationships form naturally through interaction (cooperation, conflict, competition, shared history). These connections contribute to:

- future decisions
- memory formation
- long-term personality development

As generations pass:

- inherited traits move through populations
- environmental pressures shape survival and reproduction
- lineages evolve
- communities develop distinctive traits shaped by both genetics and experience

## Emergent Narrative Promise
The stories of Aetherbourne are **not written in advance**—they emerge from the simulation itself.
