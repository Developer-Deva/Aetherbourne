# Event System
**Description:** Standardized event structure and historical logging for Aetherbourne
**Last Updated:** 2026-06-21
---

## Overview
Events are objective "facts" generated by the simulation. They represent the "what," "where," and "when" of the world's history.

---

## Event Structure
Every event consists of:
*   **Category:** (Combat, Social, Environmental, etc.)
*   **Severity:** Objective impact (0-100).
*   **Importance:** A metric for historical logging.
    `Importance = Severity × Scale × WitnessCount`
*   **Actors & Targets:** The creatures or objects involved.

---

## Event Outcomes
Outcomes are the **immediate, objective changes** to the world or creature state.
*   **Physical:** Health -10, Food +5.
*   **Social:** Relationship Points ±X (subject to interpretation by the parties involved).

---

## The Story Pipeline
Events are the raw data from which stories emerge.
`Event → Subjective Interpretation → Emotional Response → Memory`

---

## Design Philosophy
*   **Objectivity:** Events should record data, not feelings.
*   **Modularity:** Any system (Weather, AI, Combat) should be able to emit a standard Event payload.
