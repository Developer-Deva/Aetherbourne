# Flora Overview

## System Purpose

The **Flora and Botanical Systems** manage all plant species, vegetation, herbs, crops, and botanical materials that form the foundation of alchemy, medicine, sustenance, and magical systems in *Aetherbourne*.

Flora integrates with global macro drivers to procedurally generate contextually appropriate plants across multiple biomes. Custom 2D rendering properties support pixel-perfect sprite composition via a modular morphology matrix.

## Scope

- Plant matter: wild species, vegetation, herbs, crops, botanical ingredients.
- Player-facing outcomes:
  - Harvest inputs for crafting/alchemy/medicine.
  - Ecosystem effects (environmental role).
  - Naming + presentation identity.

## High-Level Concepts

- **Biome-driven plant generation**
- **Flora properties and categories** (trait taxonomy)
- **Harvest and growth lifecycle**
