# Flora Performance Optimization

## 1) Flora Management

- Growth caching
- Spatial indexing of flora locations
- Procedural generation during exploration
- LOD systems (reduce detail based on distance)
- Batch harvesting

## 2) Cultivation Efficiency

- Update all plants once per season rather than per tick
- Dormancy pooling
- Lazy evaluation (only calculate when interacted with)
- Garden snapshots to avoid recalculation
- Yield prediction to avoid runtime computation
